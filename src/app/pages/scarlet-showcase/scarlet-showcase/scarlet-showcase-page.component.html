What is Scarlet Showcase?

Scarlet Showcase is a collaborative event held at the end of each semester where creative clubs at Rutgers come together to showcase student work! Creation of Games Society (COGS), Voice Acting Club (VANCE), Video Game Music (RUCVGM), Animation Club (AC), Stitch-For-Life, Nailed It!, Rutgers Review, RU Minecraft (RUMC), and AVCA are coming together to showcase member projects and run fun activities!

Come explore club booths, grab free pizza from Gerlanda's, attend Speaker Stage segments, compete in the Dino Game Contest, and earn tickets for our gift card raffle! See the Itinerary below for more details.

Dec 3rd, 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM Busch MPR

Scarlet Showcase will be hosted inside the Multipurpose room, which is in the Busch Student Center. The event will run from 1:00 pm to 5:00 pm. A Google Map view of the location is shown for your convenience.

RSVP for the event on this event's GetInvolved page, and join the COGS discord for more information!

Itinerary

1:00 PM

Showcase + Activities

Explore engaging activities and exhibits hosted by Rutgers' creative clubs, and grab free pizza from Gerlanda's! Complete club activities and attend Speaker Stage segments to earn tickets for our $50 gift card raffle!

Event Raffle

  • Earn 1 raffle ticket for each club activity you complete (no duplicate tickets from one activity)
  • Earn 3 raffle tickets for each Speaker Stage segment you attend in full
  • Two winners will be chosen (no duplicates) — each winner receives a $50 gift card!

Event Contests

  • COGS Dino Game Contest
    • Get the highest score to win a $50 Steam gift card!
  • Other participating clubs may also host their own contests at their tables or on the Speaker Stage

Conclusion, Raffle & Contest Winners

Join us at the Speaker Stage as we wrap up Scarlet Showcase! We'll be drawing the two $50 gift card raffle winners and announcing the winner of the COGS Dino Game Contest (plus any other club contests). Stick around until the end to claim your prizes!
